AutoHotKey scripts to start background videos on the small screen.

Start different videos with F13, F14, F15 and F16

F13 - Starts videos under E:\Videos\IdleVideos
F14 - Start Spotify and plays a song in full screen
F15 - Starts videos under E:\Videos\StartScreens
F16 - Puts your computer to sleep

WARNING
Not flexible at all hehehe
will apply to MY 3rd monitor a few seconds after starting my computer.
